Welcome!

We are trying to understand how Americans make financial decisions. Often, policymakers do not fully understand people's concerns and goals. By answering this survey, you are contributing to research that can help families like yours.

This experiment has 3 parts:

  • Part 1: Questions about you and your household.
  • Part 2: Questions about how you used a Covid stimulus payment you received, or what you would do if you received some money unexpectedly.
  • Part 3: A task in which you'll be asked to categorize different actions.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by returning your submission on Prolific; however, only completed submissions can be approved for payment.

Your responses will be kept confidential and used for academic research only.

We will not collect identifying information beyond your Prolific ID, which is used only for payment and will not be linked to your responses.
