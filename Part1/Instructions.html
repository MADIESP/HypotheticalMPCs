Welcome!

We are trying to understand how Americans make financial decisions. Often, policymakers and financial planners do not fully understand people’s concerns and goals. By answering this survey, you are contributing to research that can help families like yours.

This experiment has 3 parts:

  • Part 1: Some questions about you and your household.
  • Part 2: Some questions about what you would do if you unexpectedly received some money.
  • Part 3: A task where you’ll be asked to categorize different actions. Correct answers will earn you extra bonus money.

Thank you for taking the time to participate!
