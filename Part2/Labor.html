Working time and labor income

In this section, we ask about the time that you would spend working over the three months after receiving the $1,000 payment, relative to what you would have done without the payment.

Examples of changes in working time:

  • Accepting fewer or more gigs (e.g., surveys, clients, or patients)
  • Working fewer or more extra hours for your employer
  • Taking more or fewer holidays
  • Negotiating a change in weekly hours with your employer
