Debt repayment

In this section, we ask about the amount of debt that you and your household would repay over the three months after receiving the $1,000 payment, relative to what you would have repaid without the payment.

Examples of debt repayment:

  • Credit cards (e.g., repaying a higher share of your balance)
  • Unpaid bills (e.g., utilities, medical, or legal bills)
  • Short-term loans (e.g., payday loans)
  • Longer-term loans (e.g., auto loans, student loans, mortgages)
