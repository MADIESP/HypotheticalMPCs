Savings and investments

In this section, we ask about the money that you and your household would put towards savings or investments over the three months after receiving the $1,000 payment, relative to what you would have done without the payment.

Examples of savings and investments:

  • Saving and retirement accounts: (e.g., savings accounts, retirement or pension plans, mutual funds)
  • Low-risk financial assets (e.g., life insurance products, treasury bills, government bonds, certificates of deposit)
  • Riskier financial assets (e.g., stocks, investments in the businesses of others)
  • Real estate
  • Own business investments (e.g., software, licensing fees, vehicles, machines, or other equipment for your own business)
  • Cryptocurrencies
