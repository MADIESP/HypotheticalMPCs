Suppose that today you learn that you and your household will receive an unexpected one-time payment of $1,000. You can think of this payment as a government stimulus check, tax refund, bonus, inheritance, gift, or lottery win. This one-time payment, which will not be taxed, will be available on your bank account or as a check in your mailbox within a few days.

Now consider ways in which you and your household could use this additional income over the next three months after you receive the payment:

  • Change in spending: Change in the amount of nondurable goods (e.g., food, clothes, health and beauty products, vacation, entertainment), durable goods (e.g., vehicles, furniture, household appliances), and services (e.g., babysitting, house-cleaning, tutoring) that you purchase compared to what you would have purchased without the payment.

  • Change in net assets: Change in the amount of assets that you purchase or sell (e.g., mutual funds, retirement accounts, stocks, bonds, treasury bills, life insurance, CDs, real estate, investments in own business) compared to what you would have done without the payment, resulting in a change in your total assets.

  • Change in debt repayment: Change in the amount you repay on revolving credit card debt, mortgages, student loans, auto loans, other loans, or unpaid bills (e.g., legal, medical, utilities) compared to what you would have repaid without the payment.

  • Change in uptake of new debt: Change in the amount of new debt you incur in the form of credit card debt, mortgages, student loans, auto loans, other loans, or unpaid bills compared to what you would have incurred without the payment.

  • Change in labor income caused by a change in time worked: Change in labor income caused by a change in the number of work hours, work opportunities, or gig economy jobs that you accept compared to what you would have accepted without the payment.

After adjusting your behavior in response to the payment, any remaining amount will stay in your checking account for future use.
