Part 2 – Page 1/4

Hypothetical scenario

Suppose that today you learn that you and your household will receive an unexpected one-time payment of $1,000 (for eg., a stimulus check, tax refund, bonus, inheritance, gift, or lottery win). This payment is not taxed and will arrive in your checking account within a few days.

In this part of the survey, we ask you to think about how you and your household would use this money over the next three months, compared to what you would have done if you had not received the payment.

The payment could affect your household’s financial and work-related decisions, such as:

  • how much you spend on goods and services
  • how much debt you repay
  • how much you work and earn
  • how much new debt you take on
  • how much you save or invest
