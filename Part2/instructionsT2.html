Instructions

You reported that you and your household received a Covid stimulus check of ${{participant.stimulus_amount}}.

We'll ask you to think about how you and your household used this extra money over the next three months, compared with what you would have done if you had not received the payment.

In particular, with this extra money, you and your household could have:

  • Changed your working time and earnings

    You could have changed the number of hours you worked, the work opportunities you pursued, or the gig economy jobs that you accepted, which affected both your free time and your earnings.

  • Changed your debt repayment

    You could have changed the amount of debt you repayed (e.g., credit card debt, mortgages, loans, unpaid bills).

  • Changed your uptake of new debt

    You could have changed the amount of new debt you took on (e.g., credit card debt, mortgages, loans, unpaid bills) to finance extra spending, investments and assets.

  • Changed your spending

    You could have changed your consumption of nondurable goods (e.g., food, clothes, entertainment), durable goods (e.g., vehicles, furniture), and services (e.g., babysitting, house-cleaning).

  • Changed your investments and assets

    You could have changed the amount of investments you made (e.g., real estate, investments in your own business, cryptocurrencies) and the amount of assets that you held (e.g., stocks, bonds, retirement, savings accounts).

Any amount not used could have stayed in your checking account for future use.
