New debt

In this section, we ask about the amount of new debt that you and your household would take on over the three months after receiving the $1,000 payment, relative to what you would have taken on without the payment.

What we mean by new debt:
New debt refers to debt that you take on to finance extra spending beyond what you would have been able to pay for with your current income or available savings. This can occur even if you also repay some of your existing debt during the same period.

Examples of new debt:

  • Credit cards: carrying over a higher balance to future months (for example, by using your card more for purchases and not paying the full balance immediately)
  • Unpaid bills: leaving some bills unpaid, such as medical, legal, or utility bills
  • Short-term loans: taking out new payday loans
  • Longer-term loans: taking out new auto loans, student loans, or mortgages
